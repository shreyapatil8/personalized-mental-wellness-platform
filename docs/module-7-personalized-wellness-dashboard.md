# Module 7 — Dashboard

GET /api/dashboard/me is student-owned. It supplies mood/stress averages, WHO-5 history, current-schema stress estimate, appointments, context and assistant activity.

WellnessCharts renders daily mood, reported stress and WHO-5 means with a trailing seven-calendar-day average across observed days; gaps are not treated as zero. Accessible data tables accompany charts. The score card prefers WHO-5 and otherwise scales mood to 100; it is a descriptive app score, not a new validated instrument. Eustress/Distress never manufactures a numeric score or determines clinical severity.

Recommendations are simple app prompts based on tracking and engagement, not prescriptions. Prospective usability and interpretation review remain necessary.
